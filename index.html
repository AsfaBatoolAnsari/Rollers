02 / HOW TO PLAY

LEARN IN
MINUTES.

Easy to learn. Skillful enough to keep you rolling. If you can roll a disk, you can play.

01

SET UP

Place the red and blue goals 15–20 feet apart on level grass or sand.

02

ROLL

Roll each wooden disk on its edge toward your color goal. Blue goes first.

03

SCORE

Closest to the goal scores. Side, edge, and goal contact each score differently.

04

PLAY

Keep rolling between goals. First to 21 with a two-point lead wins.
